A COOPERATIVE STORY CARD GAME — 2 TO 5 PLAYERS
PLAYER NAMES
HOW IT WORKS: Flip an Encounter card and read it aloud. Everyone must play one card from their hand — no passing. Cards are revealed simultaneously. The highest scorer claims the Spotlight, earns a trophy, and gets the success perk. The lowest scorer takes the failure penalty.

Memory cards roll 2d6 to determine their Power — average 7, range 2–12.

Ties are broken by a 2d6 roll — highest result wins.

Restricted cards are shown dimmed — they score 0 and automatically become the weakest play.

Run out of cards and you are out — no refills. Manage your hand.

Interference cards sabotage other players. Playing one uses your entire turn.
